01 / ANDROID

From download to first export.

  1. Download the APK

    Open this page in your phone browser and tap Download. If a chat app blocks it, choose Open in browser.

  2. Confirm installation

    Open the file. If prompted, allow this browser to install unknown apps, then return and tap Install. Wording varies by phone.

  3. Sign in and export

    Open RoleLeaf and sign in to Tipsy or Character.AI. Open Batch Export, choose Recent or My Characters, and select what to save.

Keep the app open during export. Device security policies can restrict direct APK installation; a QR code cannot bypass them.

02 / CHROME

A one-time local setup.

  1. Extract to a permanent folder

    Extract the ZIP into Documents or another folder you will keep. Find the tipsy-exporter folder with manifest.json inside.

  2. Enable Developer mode

    Paste this address into Chrome. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.

    chrome://extensions
  3. Load the extracted extension

    Click Load unpacked and select tipsy-exporter. Pin RoleLeaf from the puzzle-piece menu, then refresh your source website.

For desktop Chrome only. On Android, use the APK above. Managed browsers may restrict Developer mode. Do not delete or move the extension folder.

A few things worth knowing.

How do I update?

Android: download the next demo and install over the existing app. Keep the app installed to retain its sessions. Chrome: replace the contents of the same extension folder with the new files, click Reload at chrome://extensions, and refresh the source website. No store auto-updates are provided.

Does the demo need a new account?

RoleLeaf does not require its own account. You still need to sign in to Tipsy or Character.AI to access your content. Their own access rules apply.

Where do my exports go?

Exports are saved to your device. RoleLeaf has no export-upload backend. The app connects to the source websites and their media services; their privacy policies apply. ZIP files can contain private conversations and images: share them carefully.

Will everything be included?

Only data available through your current access can be exported. Creator backups and conversation exports have different coverage. Missing fields or incomplete history are listed in the ZIP report. Keep the app or browser tabs open until export finishes.
